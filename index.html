ABOUT

Welcome.

I am a PhD student in Innovation and Information Management at HKU Business School, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Zhenhui (Jack) Jiang.

My research interests lie at the intersection of AI in Business, Causal Machine Learning, and Multimodal Analytics. I study how artificial intelligence can help us understand information, behavior, and decision-making, and develop methods for prediction and policy learning in business settings.

Before joining HKU, I studied Big Data Management and Application at Xi’an Jiaotong University. Since 2024, I have also worked as a research assistant at the AI Evaluation Lab, HKU Business School.

Research interests

Research

Selected ongoing projects

MULTIMODAL ANALYTICS · SOCIAL MEDIA

Perceived Trustworthiness of Healthcare Short Videos

Understanding how users evaluate healthcare information through video content and audience responses. This project combines multimodal learning with comment analysis to predict perceived trustworthiness.

CAUSAL MACHINE LEARNING · POLICY LEARNING

Funnel-Aware Policy Learning

Learning personalized monetization policies from randomized experiments in an online game. The framework models the exposure–click–purchase funnel and uses orthogonal estimation to support policy evaluation and optimization.

GENERATIVE AI · INFORMATION RETRIEVAL

Generative Engine Optimization

Investigating how webpage content influences citations in AI-generated answers, with an emphasis on causal evaluation and the semantic relationships between target pages and authoritative sources.

LLM AGENTS · FINANCIAL DECISION-MAKING

Multi-Agent Financial Decision-Making

Exploring how specialized AI agents can contribute complementary market, macroeconomic, and news perspectives to financial decisions, and how to select among their proposed strategies.
