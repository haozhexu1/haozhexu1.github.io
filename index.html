I am a PhD student in Innovation and Information Management at HKU Business School, advised by Prof. Zhenhui (Jack) Jiang.

My research explores how humans, AI, and organizations interact in AI-mediated environments. I am interested in AI in Business, Human–AI Interaction, Causal Machine Learning, and Multimodal Analytics, spanning behavioral and design science research in Information Systems.
