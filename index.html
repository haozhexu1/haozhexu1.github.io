ABOUT

Welcome.

I am a PhD student in Innovation and Information Management at HKU Business School, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Zhenhui (Jack) Jiang.

My research interests lie at the intersection of AI in Business, Causal Machine Learning, and Multimodal Analytics, spanning both behavioral and design science research in Information Systems. I am particularly interested in understanding the behaviors and interactions of humans, AI, and organizations in AI-mediated environments, and in designing computational methods and AI systems to better understand, predict, and support decision-making in business contexts.

Before joining HKU, I studied Big Data Management and Application at Xi’an Jiaotong University. Since 2024, I have also worked as a research assistant at the AI Evaluation Lab, HKU Business School.
