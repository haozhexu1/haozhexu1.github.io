Haozhe Xu

Innovation and Information Management

HKU Business School / The University of Hong Kong

I am a first-year PhD student in Innovation and Information Management at HKU Business School, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Zhenhui (Jack) Jiang.

My research primarily focuses on Design Science Research in Information Systems, where I develop novel machine learning, causal machine learning, and large language model (LLM)-based methods to address challenges in prediction, decision-making, and optimization in business contexts.

I am also actively exploring Human–AI Interaction and Behavioral Information Systems, with a particular interest in understanding how individuals interact with AI systems and how these interactions shape human behavior and decision-making.
